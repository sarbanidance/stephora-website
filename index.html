About Us

Art is a guarantee of sanity

Stephora Classical Dance School is led by Sarbani Ghosh, an accomplished Indian classical dancer specializing in Bharatanatyam and contemporary Indian dance forms. A dedicated disciple of Guru Smt. Anita Mallick, she has trained extensively in the rich traditions of Indian classical dance.

Sarbani has choreographed and performed at numerous prestigious dance festivals in India and abroad, including the International Dance Festival in Bhubaneswar and Banga Sammelan (North American Bengali Conference – NABC) in Las Vegas.

Now based in Morrisville, North Carolina, she brings this rich artistic heritage to the Triangle community, nurturing each student through the traditional Guru–Shishya Parampara with discipline, grace, creativity, and joy.

“Dance is the hidden language of the soul.”
