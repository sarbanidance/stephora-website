Your Guru · Sarbani Ghosh

Meet Your Guru

Sarbani Ghosh

Classical dancer · Choreographer · Teacher

Stephora Classical Dance School is led by Sarbani Ghosh, an accomplished Indian classical dancer specializing in Bharatanatyam and contemporary Indian dance forms. A dedicated disciple of Guru Smt. Anita Mallick, she has trained extensively in the rich traditions of Indian classical dance.

Sarbani has choreographed and performed at numerous prestigious dance festivals in India and abroad, including the International Dance Festival in Bhubaneswar and Banga Sammelan (North American Bengali Conference – NABC) in Las Vegas.

Now based in Morrisville, North Carolina, she brings this rich artistic heritage to the Triangle community, nurturing each student through the traditional Guru–Shishya Parampara with discipline, grace, creativity, and joy.

“Dance is the hidden language of the soul.”

A Structured Learning Pathway

A foundation that grows with the dancer

Bharatanatyam at Stephora is taught as a progressive curriculum — not just a class — so every dancer advances with a clear path.

1

Foundation

Level 1
  • Posture & body alignment
  • Basic adavus
  • Hand gestures
  • Simple rhythms & musical awareness
  • Basic expressions
Goal: build a strong, enjoyable foundation.
2

Development

Level 2
  • More complex adavus
  • Jathis & rhythmic patterns
  • Abhinaya (expression)
  • Traditional compositions & repertoire
  • Performance preparation
Goal: develop technical control & artistry.
3

Performance

Level 3
  • Advanced repertoire
  • Complex rhythm
  • Deeper abhinaya
  • Choreography & stagecraft
  • Solo & group performance
Goal: become a confident, expressive performer.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need previous dance experience?

No. Stephora welcomes complete beginners as well as students with previous experience. Every student is guided according to their current level and learning needs.

What age can students start Bharatanatyam?

Stephora offers classes for children, teens and adults. Contact us with the student’s age and experience level and we’ll recommend the right class.

Do adults need previous dance experience?

No. Adults who are new to Bharatanatyam are welcome to inquire about beginner opportunities.

What will students learn?

Students learn Bharatanatyam fundamentals including adavus, mudras, rhythm (tala), abhinaya and repertoire, progressing toward advanced technique and performance.

Where are classes held?

Classes are held at Stephora Classical Dance School, 10290 Chapel Hill Rd, Suite 200, Morrisville, NC — near Sangam Mart.

Which days are classes offered?

Bharatanatyam is offered Monday or Wednesday, 6:00–7:00 PM. Choreography is offered Thursday, 7:30–8:30 PM for the senior batch.

Can I try a class before enrolling?

Yes — Stephora offers a free demo class for prospective students. Contact us to reserve your spot.

Is Bharatanatyam only for girls?

No. Bharatanatyam is for anyone interested in learning it. Students of all ages and backgrounds are welcome.
